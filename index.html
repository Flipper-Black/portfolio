# Why we started Barcona

I co-founded Barcona Systems with Kenan to turn our shared interest in software and engineering into something practical. Barcona brings computer vision and automation to warehouse inventory, connecting what a camera sees with information people can use.

# What I enjoy about it

I like working where code has a visible effect in the real world. With Barcona, that means bringing software, cameras, and engineering together, testing ideas, and improving them until the pieces work as a system.

Building it with Kenan makes that process even more interesting: sharing ideas, solving problems together, and learning as we go. That mix of curiosity, collaboration, and hands-on work is what draws me to projects like this.
