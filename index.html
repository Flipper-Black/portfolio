Info about Barcona Systems+

BARCONA AI turns warehouse camera images into barcode values, shelf assignments and status flags. It is designed for cameras on existing forklifts or an autonomous scanning robot, helping keep track of inventory during everyday operations.

GYPT — Magnetic Carousel

An ongoing experimental physics project at Phaenovum. More details after completion.

  • Experimental Physics
  • Magnetism

Info about GYPT — Magnetic Carousel+

A GYPT research project at Phaenovum exploring a steel ball above a rotating disc of alternating magnets. The experiment investigates why the ball can travel with or against the disc’s rotation, and how changing the setup affects its motion.

HeronShield

An AI-based water sprayer that detects herons and helps protect fish ponds.

  • Computer Vision
  • AI
  • Automation
Info about HeronShield+

A camera monitors the pond while AI identifies approaching herons. When a heron is detected, the system activates a directed spray of water to deter it and help protect the fish.

ZeroXWaste

A camera-based sorting bin that directs waste into the right compartment.

  • Computer Vision
  • Robotics
  • Sustainability
Info about ZeroXWaste+

A camera identifies waste placed on a sorting plate and assigns it to one of four categories: organic waste, lightweight packaging (Gelber Sack), residual waste or paper. The plate then turns towards the corresponding bin to guide the item into the right compartment.

Automated waste sorting
